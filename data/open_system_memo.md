# Round 8 — Open-system / ongoing-nucleation track: memo

**Verdict: AGAINST the tested prediction (the closed-system fluidization picture does not transfer to an open, continuously nucleating tissue).** On the same 192×64 lattice, s_d = 0.01, the closed two-clone half-domain system reproduces the paper's load-driven fluidization — median T_fix / own-neutral-anchor = 0.752 [0.676, 0.872], 0.550 [0.494, 0.616], 0.259 [0.234, 0.291] at θ = U_d/s_d = 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, crossing the ½ ridge criterion between θ = 0.4 and 0.8 — but in the open system with ongoing lineage nucleation (ν = 5×10⁻⁴ per birth, ~65–70 coexisting lineages, 400 loaded runs/rung, 600-run own neutral anchor in every arm), the matched coarsening observable is essentially unmoved: cohort half-life / neutral = 0.992 [0.987, 0.998], 0.986 [0.980, 0.994], 0.983 [0.977, 0.989], and cohort extinction time / neutral = 1.00, 0.825 [0.75, 0.91], 0.75 [0.68, 0.83]. At θ = 0.8 — roughly twice the freeze/flow ridge — the closed system is accelerated 3.9-fold while the open system is accelerated ≤1.3-fold, and none of the open-system observables crosses ½ anywhere on the ladder. A single fitness-matched competitor nucleated inside a loaded resident field (2,000 runs/rung single-cell + 400 loaded/600 neutral disc runs, r = 3) shows survival-probability ratios to neutral consistent with 1 (or, at the top load, *below* 1): single-cell P(≥32)/neutral = 0.91–1.16 (CIs span 1); disc P(≥256)/neutral = 1.02 [0.71, 1.43], 1.02 [0.70, 1.45], 0.59 [0.37, 0.89] at θ = 0.2, 0.4, 0.8 — the only significant deviation is a *suppression* of nucleated-clone survival under heavy load, the opposite of a fluidization boost. The reviewer objection stands as a genuine limitation: the fluidization mechanism requires two macroscopic clones that *diverge* in load over ~10⁵–10⁶ frames; a lineage nucleated inside a steady-state loaded field is born fitness-matched to its neighbours and never acquires that between-clone asymmetry, so the load-driven flow signature is nearly erased.

## What was tested and how

Convention throughout: θ = U_d/s_d = μ_d/s_d, U_d = μ_d with no factor of 2; here s_d = 0.01, θ ∈ {0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8} (μ_d ∈ {0, 0.002, 0.004, 0.008}); death–Birth Moran, Moore-8, dt = 2400, mean lifetime 1.16×10⁻⁵ (p_die = 0.0275/frame), 192×64 strip (N = 12,288), periodic y, walled x. Every arm carries its own ≥600-run neutral (θ = 0) anchor built fresh; every loaded number below is a ratio to that arm's own anchor.

Kernel: `evolution_open.py`, a self-contained numba kernel implementing the identical per-event dynamics of the bundle's `evolution_strip.py` (deaths by alias-geometric Bernoulli gaps; fitness-weighted refill from the frozen post-death state; deleterious counter decremented by 2 per division, multiplicative ×(1−s_d)). Validation: (i) in two-clone mode with no burn-in and ν = 0 it is **bit-identical** to the reference kernel — equal t_fix, winner, final label field and final fitness field at seeds 7, 11, 3 on 64×32, 96×32, 64×64; (ii) neutral single-cell fixation probability on 64×32 = 31/60,000 = 5.17×10⁻⁴ ± 0.93×10⁻⁴ vs the exact Moran value 1/N = 4.88×10⁻⁴ (z = 0.31); (iii) label bookkeeping conserves cells (Σ lineage sizes = live cells; grid recount matches the counter).

All arms use a **300,000-frame unlabelled burn-in** so the resident field is at its load steady state (mean fitness relative to a fresh field: 0.996, 0.992, 0.963 at θ = 0.2, 0.4, 0.8) *before* any lineage is marked. This is a deliberate design choice and the crux of the interpretation (below).

**Experiment 1 — coarsening under nucleation (E1).** After burn-in every live cell becomes a distinct lineage; thereafter each birth founds a new lineage with probability ν = 5×10⁻⁴ (chosen from a pilot so that ~50–200 lineages coexist; observed stationary K ≈ 69 neutral). 400 loaded runs per rung plus a 600-run neutral (θ = 0) anchor, 2×10⁶ frames observation. Observables: number of distinct lineages present, surviving members of the t = 0 cohort, cohort half-life t₁/₂ and cohort extinction time, stationary K.

**Experiment 2 — single nucleated competitor (E2a/E2b).** In the burnt-in loaded field, one centre cell (E2a, 2,000 runs/rung; f_marker = f_field, i.e. neutral marking) or a radius-3 disc (E2b, m₀ ≈ 29; 400 loaded runs/rung, 600-run neutral anchor) is relabelled and run to absorption; observables: probability of ever reaching size m, and conditional takeover kinetics.

**Closed-system reference (CS).** Half-domain two-clone T_fix on the *same* 192×64 lattice, 600-run neutral anchor + 300/rung, to make the open-vs-closed comparison like-for-like rather than against the deposited 192×32 numbers.

Load does *not* accelerate the coarsening of the t = 0 cohort (t₁/₂ shifts by −0.8 %, −1.4 %, −1.7 %); the whole survivor curves (fig. c) are near-identical over three decades of time. The only load response is a modest depression of the stationary lineage number K (−5.5 %, −10.5 %, −18.0 %) and a matching modest shortening of the deep tail of the cohort extinction time (−17.5 %, −25 %) — a signature of *purifying selection culling load-heavy sub-lineages*, not of interface fluidization: it appears at θ = 0.2 already (below the ridge), scales smoothly rather than switching on at the ridge, and never approaches the ½ criterion. Where the closed system shows a 3.9× speed-up at θ = 0.8, the open system shows 1.02× (half-life) / 1.34× (extinction tail).

The θ = 0.8 disc arm shows a statistically significant *reduction* in survival to macroscopic size (the marked disc inherits the loaded field's fitness spread and its heavier-loaded members are culled), the opposite of a fluidization boost. Full-field takeover from a nucleated seed is a ~1/N event (0/8,000 single-cell runs; 4/1,800 disc runs fixed: 2 neutral, 2 at θ = 0.2, 0 at θ = 0.4 and 0.8), so the conditional takeover-time comparison is unpowered and is reported as inconclusive rather than as evidence either way.

## Interpretation — why the open system erases the effect

In the closed two-clone geometry both clones start at fitness 1 and each half accumulates load *independently*; over ~10⁵–10⁶ frames their fitness distributions diverge (drift + asynchronous Muller clicks), and it is this **between-clone load asymmetry** that mobilises the interface. In the open system, a new lineage inherits its parent's fitness — it is born a fitness-matched member of a field already at the mutation–selection–drift steady state, where the within-field fitness variance is tiny (E[f²]−E[f]² ≈ 4×10⁻⁵, 8×10⁻⁵, 1.6×10⁻⁴ at θ = 0.2, 0.4, 0.8). Competing lineages therefore never develop the macroscopic fitness asymmetry that drives the closed-system flow; each lineage boundary experiences the *same* steady-state field on both sides. The remaining, weak load dependence is the ordinary purifying-selection reduction of the effective neutral coalescent (K falls a little), which is monotone, present below the ridge, and has no ½ crossing. **The freeze/flow ridge is a property of a sharp macroscopic boundary between two independently ratcheting populations, not of load per se.** This directly supports the reviewer's objection: in a tissue where clones nucleate continuously as single cells, the load-driven fluidization/freeze bar is not predicted to appear.

A weaker, defensible salvage for the paper (offered adversarially, not endorsed): the closed-system prediction should survive wherever the biology *does* create two extended, independently evolving compartments meeting at a boundary (grafts, chimeras, clonally expanded fields meeting after long separate histories). It does not apply to homeostatic single-cell nucleation, which is the situation the reviewer names. The paper would need to restrict its scope accordingly, or add a mechanism that lets nucleated clones diverge in load faster than they coalesce.

## Caveats / limits of this track

- The load dependence that *is* seen (K −18 % at θ = 0.8; cohort extinction tail −25 %) is monotone in θ and could be read as "load accelerates coarsening"; it is quantitatively an order of magnitude weaker than the closed-system effect, does not reach the ½ criterion, and is not localised at the ridge — so it does not rescue the freeze/flow picture, but it is not literally zero either.
- ν = 5×10⁻⁴ was set by pilot to give ~50–200 coexisting lineages; a single ν rung is deposited. Very much smaller ν (few, large, long-lived lineages) interpolates toward the closed two-clone limit and may recover part of the effect — this crossover in ν was not mapped (natural follow-up).
- Marker mode marks a competitor of *identical* fitness inside the loaded field; a competitor born at fitness 1 into a load-depressed field (i.e. a locally load-free mutant) is a different question (selective sweep of an unloaded clone) and is not the fluidization signature; not tested.
- Cohort-extinction-time medians are grid-quantised (24 samples/decade); the θ = 0.2 ratio of exactly 1.000 [0.908, 1.000] is a grid artefact — the un-quantised cohort half-life (0.991 [0.985, 0.998]) is the primary statistic.
- Takeover-time comparison is unpowered (4 disc fixations across 1,800 runs); only survival probabilities are decisive in E2.
- Seed audit (computed; see `seed_audit.cross_check_computed` in the summary JSON): 13,100 production runs (12,700 initial + 200-run neutral top-ups in the E1 and E2b arms so every arm's neutral anchor is ≥600 runs), unique seeds within 13,520,000–13,547,499 (E1 13,520,000–13,521,799; E2a 13,530,000–13,537,999; E2b 13,540,000–13,541,799; CS 13,546,000–13,547,499), disjoint per arm. Exact set-intersection against every `seed` column deposited in the round-8 bundle (`aspect_ladder`, `reduced_model_runs`, `ridge_profile_runs`, `ridge_profile_analysis_pool`): 0 shared seeds. Against SEEDS.md: every documented range and every documented round-6/7 seed base (9.4M, 12.0M, 12.64M, 12.9M) lies strictly below 13,520,000, EXCEPT the two legacy tables `bd_results.parquet` (200,150–2,146,924,700; 2,900 unique) and `tail_ensemble.parquet` (1,256,982–2,146,924,700; 1,600 unique), whose documented ranges span the whole block; their per-run seed lists are not shipped in the round-8 bundle, so exact disjointness against those two tables could not be verified here and is disclosed as unverified rather than claimed (their ~4,500 seeds are scattered over a 2.1×10⁹-wide range; different kernels, parameters and geometry; never compared or pooled with this deposit). Disclosure: throwaway pilot A (neutral fixation-probability check) consumed 60,000 seeds and spilled past the block ceiling (13,559,000–13,618,999), and 108 pilot-B seeds coincide with pilot-A seed integers (different geometry/parameters, never compared or pooled) — pilots are deposited flagged as pilots and enter no headline statistic; the next track allocating from 13,560,000 should skip to ≥13,619,000.
- No run cap was hit; censored count = 0 in every arm (single-cell and disc caps were 4×10⁷ frames; CS cap 6×10⁷).
