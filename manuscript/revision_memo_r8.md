# Revision memo — round 8

Governing document: unchanged from round 7 — `preregistration/prereg_r7.md` in the public release governs the criterion tests; the four round-8 computational tracks below were run as adversarial tests with the outcome (for / against) reported before integration. Round-7 panel: 48 major / 2 minor, zero fatal, zero reject; the panel's own diagnosis was that ~60% of major concerns traced to a single framing fact — the paper's headline was the criterion it had three times failed to derive. This memo maps each round-7 charge to what was done.

## The result of round 8, stated first

The paper is reframed onto its robust core, and its remaining generality claims were each tested.

- **The reframe.** Title, abstract, the criterion Results heading, Fig. 4's title and Table 2's caption now present the freeze bar as a *measured* order-unity ratio whose explanation is open, with the Haigh correspondence stated as an analogy in the control parameter and the class-extinction mechanism excluded by the paper's own census. The self-assessment sentence naming the failed derivations as the paper's "strongest evidence" is deleted; in its place a standalone paragraph confronts the three failed derivations and adopts "measured, explanation open" as the paper's position. A new **Table 3** lists the thirteen load-bearing claims, states for each whether it was adversarially tested here and what happened, and names the falsifying observation for every claim that was not.
- **Four adversarial tests, all reported by outcome.** (1) *Lattice topology (FOR):* on hexagonal and random-coordination lattices the freeze bar sits at U_d/s_d = 0.33 [0.24, 0.38] and 0.34 [0.29, 0.44], inside the Moore/von Neumann envelope, with the four stencils' ridges ordering as their interface coordination orders — the bar is not a square-lattice artefact (Supplementary Fig. S29; 3,072 runs). (2) *Placement propagation (FOR):* propagating every tissue-parameter box jointly with the bar's own heterogeneity puts the colonic crypt fluid with probability 0.9999 and the other tissues at 1.0000; the earlier single-corner "colon marginal, +0.13 decades" was a corner of joint probability ≈10⁻⁴ and is retired; the pooled anchor is stable to leave-one-out of any neutral sub-ensemble (bar shift ≤ 0.007) (Supplementary Fig. S27). (3) *Ridge-law refit and low-s_d extension (FOR the leading order, with an ADVERSE bound):* re-anchoring the traced ridge law on the pooled neutral ensemble leaves k = 1 the selected form (k = 1.15 ± 0.13, ΔAIC −0.14 versus free) but a run-level bootstrap excludes exactly 1 while OLS includes it, and the two ladder families carry different local exponents, both disclosed; a new rung at s_d = 0.00125 (1,464 runs) finds *no* crossing within U_d/s_d ≤ 1.6 — the trough's left arm keeps rising, so the freeze bar is order-unity only for s_d ≳ 2 × 10⁻³, now stated as a limit in the abstract, the profile passage and Table 3, with the placements shown geometrically unaffected (Supplementary Fig. S28). (4) *Open-system nucleation (AGAINST the closed-system extrapolation):* on the same 192 × 64 lattice a closed two-clone system fluidizes (T_fix/neutral 0.26 at U_d/s_d = 0.8) while a continuously nucleating field's cohort half-life stays within 2% of neutral and a nucleated competitor is not advantaged (disc survival ratio 0.59 at the top load) — fluidization requires a between-clone load asymmetry that a lineage born inside a loaded field never acquires; the paper's domain is now stated as established two-compartment boundaries, in the abstract, a new Results subsection, the Discussion and Table 3 (Supplementary Fig. S30; 13,100 runs).
- **The correction register now has six entries**, each a claim of a previous version that a test designed to fail did fail, or bounded.
- **Consolidated limits.** A single "What this study has not tested" paragraph in the Discussion now lists the geometry, dynamics, interface-statistics, genealogy and data boundaries the study did not cross, replacing distributed caveats.

## What was not done, and why it is stated rather than done

An off-lattice (Voronoi/vertex-model) or curved-sheet geometry, an event-driven asynchronous reference kernel, a nucleation-rate sweep to the few-large-clone limit, an unloaded (sweep-type) competitor test, a sub-10⁻³ effect-size rung, and any wet-lab or boundary-kinematic dataset are outside this revision. Each is named — with the observation that would falsify the corresponding claim — in the Discussion's limits paragraph and in Table 3, so the tested and untested edges of the model are stated in one place rather than left as silent gaps.
